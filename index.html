WHO WE ARE

A Goa-based production house, working across three cities.

Pulse Society Goa is an event design and production company headquartered in Goa, with standing teams and vendor relationships in Bangalore and Mumbai. We're not an agency that subcontracts everything out — we run stage, sound, décor, hospitality and permits ourselves, so the same crew that plans your event is the one standing behind the desk on the night itself. Nine seasons in, we still do it at a sussegado pace — unhurried, personal — while delivering with zero-delay production discipline.
